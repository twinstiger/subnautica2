Item Encyclopedia


Full crafting recipes and usage for every item in Subnautica 2. Resources → Tools → Equipment → Consumables.

⛏️ Resources
Item Type Location / How to Get Usage
Titanium Raw Debris around pod. Rocks. Common everywhere. Base crafting, tools, batteries
Copper Raw Underwater caves, rock formations. Moderate depth. Scanner, Battery, wires
Quartz Raw Pink/orange coral formations. Angel Comb area. Scanner, Habitat Builder
Silver Raw Shallow caves north of pod, 290-350m depth. Flippers, O2 Tank, advanced tools
Gold Raw Deeper caves. Requires O2 upgrades to reach. Habitat Builder, premium equipment
Lead Raw Near thermal vents, mid-depth caves. Battery, radiation equipment
Lithium Raw Mid-to-deep caves. Unlock after first chapters. Biomod crafting, high-tier tools
Sulfur Raw Thermal vent areas. Heat-resistant zones. Acid-related crafting, batteries
🔧 Tools
ItemTypeRecipeUsage
Survival Multitool Tool 3× Titanium Cut plants, break rocks, basic interaction. Always carry it.
Scanner Tool 2 Titanium + 2 Quartz + 1 Acidic Raion Pod Scan fragments to unlock blueprints. Most important tool.
Habitat Builder Tool 2 Titanium + 1 Copper + 1 Gold Place base modules. Foundation of your underwater home.
Bio Scanner Tool 2 Copper + 1 Silver + 1 Titanium Identify creatures and scan biological samples.
Sonar Resonator Tool 3 Copper + 2 Gold + 1 Battery Locate points of interest. Essential for navigation.
Flashlight Tool 1 Titanium + 1 Glass + 1 Battery Illuminate dark caves and deep zones.
🦾 Equipment
ItemTypeRecipeBenefit
Flippers Equipment 2× Rubber + 1× Titanium +50% swim speed. Essential for exploration.
Standard O2 Tank Equipment 2× Rubber + 1× Titanium +30s oxygen capacity. Required for deeper zones.
Reinforced Suit Equipment 4× Titanium + 2× Lead + 1× Gold Pressure resistance. Unlocks deep ocean zones.
Headlamp Equipment 1× Titanium + 1× Glass + 1× Wire Hands-free lighting. Critical for cave exploration.
🥤 Consumables
ItemTypeRecipe / SourceEffect
Water Consumable 1× Water Slug → Fabricator Only early-game freshwater source. Thirst kills fast.
Nutrition Block Consumable 1× Biofuel Block + 1× Salt → Processor Sustained hunger recovery. Best long-term food.
First Aid Kit Consumable 2× Healing Slime + 1× Anti-corrosive Heals damage from predator attacks.
Biofuel Block Consumable 2× Organic Matter → Fabricator Power base systems. Burnable energy source.
Attractant Flare Consumable 1× Glow Moss + 1× Sulfur Lures fish for easier collection. Useful for farming.
